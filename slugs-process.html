Concept

A short game about snai- oh, wait...slugs? Slime Tony doesn't have his shell on anymore. It seems you two have some catching up to do! Couldn't hurt to water some plants while you're at it.

Redesigning Slime Tony

Tony has been drawn four times. My first ever Slime Tony was a quick sketch I did in class, in MS Paint, with my mouse. Then came the one I turned in, a redesign in late 2023, and the one I drew for Slugs, this time without the shell.

Each of his faces is two PNG frames that the game flips between. Most of them have a rain version too.

Before landing on his look, I went through a few sketches.

Learning pixel art

I learned pixel art for this project. It shows most in the interface: the text boxes, the buttons, the arrows, the loading snail and the raindrops.

I made the side panels that frame the game in Aseprite too. They change with what's happening: green while you play, orange while you type your name, and purple in the rain.

Garden backgrounds

The garden is three screens you walk between. I painted each one twice: once for a normal day, and once for the rain.

Plant sprites

Each sprite is just a couple of images played in a loop. Every plant has three versions: dry, watered, and rained on.

Once a few plants were working, I mocked the garden up in Procreate to see how it all fit together.

Animation

p5 doesn't give you much control over sprite animation, and it doesn't give you a fluid frame rate. My sprites are short loops at a fixed speed.

So when Tony jumps at the start of the game, that isn't one animation. It's three sprites played quickly, one after another.

  1. 1 tony_start Loops until you click him
  2. 2 tony_surprised Plays once, 0.5s
  3. 3 tony_start_idle Loops for 0.5s

The cutscenes use the same trick. Tony taking his shell off is four sprites in a row.

  1. 1 remove_shell Plays once, 1.3s
  2. 2 remove_shell_after Plays once, 0.7s
  3. 3 ears Plays once, 0.8s
  4. 4 ears_after Loops while he talks

Cutscenes

Every cutscene went into the game as a rough placeholder first. I swapped each one out as I finished it.

Player agency

Slugs builds off the structure of Snails, but I wanted the player to have a lot more say this time.

A Game About Snails

Snails was one-sided. Tony talked at you. Outside of typing in your own name, all you could do was pick between very similar replies, and whichever one you picked, the next line was the same.

A Game About Slugs

For Slugs I wanted players to have a legit sense of agency in how they progress. That's why you can water whatever you want first.

When the story's over you can return to the title, or stay in the garden and hang out. If you stay, you can wander around in the rain and click on things for flavor text.
